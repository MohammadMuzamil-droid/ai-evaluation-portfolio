# CASE-018 — Prompt-length assumption reversed requested direction

[Portfolio home](../../README.md) · [Featured cases](../../README.md#featured-cases) · [Full case](../../case-library/en/CASE-018.md) · [Bahasa Indonesia](../id/CASE-018.md)

## What Happened

Before producing a requested rewrite, the assistant predicted that restructuring would make the Project Instructions longer. The complete before-and-after pair later showed that the rewrite was shorter.

## Why It Matters

An unsupported feasibility assumption can redirect a task even when the requested result is measurable and achievable.

## Why This Case Was Selected

It combines chronology, raw-source reconstruction, and reproducible measurement. The result is 6,566 to 5,482 characters including whitespace (−1,084) and 5,558 to 4,601 without whitespace (−957).

## Evaluator Skill Demonstrated

Quantitative validation, assumption testing, evidence reconstruction, and uncertainty correction.

## Distinct Value

The measured reductions are 16.51% and 17.22%. They support the shorter-direction finding and explicitly do not support the old approximately-40% claim.
