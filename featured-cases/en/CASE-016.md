# CASE-016 — Document generated before uncertainty resolved

[Portfolio home](../../README.md) · [Featured cases](../../README.md#featured-cases) · [Full case](../../case-library/en/CASE-016.md) · [Bahasa Indonesia](../id/CASE-016.md)

## What Happened

A document was generated while identity or extraction uncertainty was still open. The correction stated that confirmation should have happened before file creation.

## Why It Matters

Creating a polished artefact can turn an unresolved assumption into something that looks final and may be used before the uncertainty is noticed.

## Why This Case Was Selected

It gives a clear example of a missing decision gate in a consequential workflow.

## Evaluator Skill Demonstrated

Assumption detection, uncertainty handling, missing-requirement analysis, and tool-execution review.

## Distinct Value

The case focuses on execution timing. The evidence supports premature generation, not a claim that the final document contained a specific factual error.
