## Evaluation Question

Did the documentation workflow preserve approved content and use available project sources before generation or a request to resubmit?

## Evidence Chain and Chronology

The approved case consolidates `EVENT-0329`, `EVENT-0335`, and `EVENT-0354`. Each sequence retains the governing context, response, correction, and follow-up. The complete chain maps to `CASE-014-E01` through `CASE-014-E12`. It connects three stages: approved source, source retrieval, and generated artefact.

## Evaluator Reasoning

An approval does not end the fidelity test. The approved text becomes the reference. Available project material is the first retrieval route. The generated artefact must then be checked against that reference, with any intentional omission disclosed.

## Observation and Inference

**Observation:** approved wording or available sources were not carried through the workflow consistently. **Bounded inference:** the approval-to-export lineage was weak. The record does not reconstruct every omitted sentence or prove that every output was wholly unusable.

## Competing Interpretation

A generated document may still be useful or well formatted. That does not resolve the evaluation question. Usefulness is different from fidelity to the approved source and the required retrieval order.

## Uncertainty and Impact

The exact extent of every content difference is not quantified here. The supported impact is that a user may approve one version but receive, or be asked to recreate, another.

## Lesson for Evaluation Practice

Document evaluation should follow source lineage across approval, retrieval, generation, and delivery. A final-file check without the approved reference can miss content loss.
